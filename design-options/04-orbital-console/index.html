About

Research, markets, and long-distance focus.

Hello. I am Xunhe Andrew Zhang, a Master's student in Financial Engineering at Columbia University and a researcher in astronomy on the MDW H-alpha survey.

I completed undergraduate studies at Columbia in Astronomy and Economics-Mathematics, with earlier work on exoplanet and exomoon simulations.

Outside academics, I train for marathons, spend time in the gym, and live with two cats, Nebula and Polaris.

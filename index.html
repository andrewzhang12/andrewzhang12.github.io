About Me

Hello there! I'm Xunhe (Andrew) Zhang. I am a Post-Baccalaureate researcher in the Department of Astronomy at Columbia University in NYC. I am currently working on the MDW H-alpha survey project as a researcher. Before this, I completed my undergraduate program at Columbia University in Astronomy and Economics-Mathematics, where I have worked on exoplanets and exomoon simulations. I grew up in Beijing, China before moving to Pebble Beach, CA where I attended high school.

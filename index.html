About Me

Hello! I'm Xunhe (Andrew) Zhang. I am currently a Master’s student in Financial Engineering at Columbia University, while continuing my work as a researcher in astronomy on the MDW H-alpha survey.

I completed my undergraduate studies at Columbia in Astronomy and Economics–Mathematics, where I previously worked on exoplanet and exomoon simulations. I grew up in Beijing, China, and later moved to Pebble Beach, California for high school.

Outside of academics, I’m into endurance sports—especially marathon training—and I also spend time in the gym. I live with my two cats, Nebula and Polaris. I’m also naturally interested in technology: I used to spend a lot of time building PCs and optimizing my gaming setup—geeky, but not quite the “tech news every morning” type.

For a more detailed overview of my academic background and experience, a full PDF version of my résumé can be downloaded here. Details on my research projects and publications are available in the sections below.
